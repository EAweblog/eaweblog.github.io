The cause of the declining fertility rate

I have my own explanation for the steady decline in the fertility rate from 1960 to today. My explanation is not a rejection of others' explanations for the decline, but a re-emphasis of what I see as the most important factors, from my own lived experience, and from the observation that fertility declines are almost all from the decline in cohort fertility of women in their 20s, not from women in their 30s. I call it the sidequest theory of low fertility. Compare life today to life in 1960. There are many more sidequests available. Most people can go to college for 4 years and grad school for another 6 years. Any woman can enter a career and work 50-60 hours a week if she wants. There is an inexhaustible number of videogames and TV shows. You can doomscroll for 6 hours a day. So people can just do the sidequests their whole life and never get bored, perpetually postponing, or avoiding, the main quest of marriage and children.

Money is not really an important factor in reproduction. If money were an important factor, it would be difficult to explain why subsistence farmers in Africa routinely have 6+ children. Women can reproduce any time they want on basically any budget. The limiting factor on number of children for women is how much time they DON'T spend on sidequests like education and career. This is a much better explanation for why 3rd worlders with no resources and nothing to do have lots of children. And it explains how 1st world women with inexhaustible opportunities to go to college and have an important big girl job in government or a high income office job (in other words, have way too many things to do) have basically no children.

Also for men, money is not really the important factor in reproduction. Men (in civilized society) can only reproduce by inducing a woman to cooperate in reproduction. It's easier for men to do this when they have more money, but money isn't actually the cause. Money is just a proxy for status, and status is what a man uses to induce a woman to reproduce.
